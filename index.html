⏳ Tenses in English 🇬🇧

⏰ Time is essential for everyone and everything, and in English, we have as many as twelve tenses to precisely define when something happens! 🕰️ Each tense has its unique function, helping us express actions in the present, past, and future. 🌟

🔄 Whether you're talking about routines 🛍️, ongoing events 📖, past memories 🕊️, or plans for tomorrow 🌅 – every English tense has its place and purpose! ✅
